New system

Emtaş automatic-feed solid fuel apartment boilers stand out through their 4-pass flame reversal and water-jacketed flue. They are built with advanced technology to TS EN 303-5 and burn all types of coal, olive pomace, hazelnut shells and similar solid fuels up to 20 mm in size.

Energy saving

The automatic temperature control runs and stops the boiler at the set temperature. The fully digital control panel meters the combustion air precisely, so the boiler keeps heating in a controlled way and the room temperature stays constant. When the fuel runs out, the system switches off automatically.

High efficiency

Emtaş automatic-feed solid fuel apartment boilers turn the flame 4 times inside the boiler with the 4-pass flame reversal system and keep it in the firebox longer. The water-jacketed flue also lowers the temperature of the exhaust gas considerably. This gets the most out of the fuel.

Ideal combustion

Fuel is fed into the boiler from below by an auger, so problems such as the fire going out are eliminated, and combustion air from the surrounding walls of the firebox burns the fuel evenly and ideally.

Assurance

Emtaş automatic-feed 4-pass solid fuel boilers are built to meet the heating needs of homes and workplaces of every kind and provide safe, economical heating. Our products are TSE and CE certified and come with a 2-year warranty.
